## Who I Am

I do not build simple apps or dashboards.

I design and engineer execution-level AI systems that:
- Understand business operations
- Make decisions
- Execute workflows autonomously

From ERP/MES platforms to multi-agent orchestration engines, my mission is converting companies into intelligent execution systems.

## Core Focus

```text
Autonomous AI systems (not copilots)
Multi-agent orchestration
Business process execution (end-to-end)
AI-native enterprise architecture
SaaS multi-tenant systems at scale
```

## Key Systems

### Orion (EndexAI)
Autonomous AI operating across web, email, and bots.

- Observe -> reason -> act -> persist
- Executes real tasks, not only responses
- Persistent memory and decision loop

### EndexAgents
Multi-agent business execution framework.

- Lead detection -> scoring -> contact -> proposal -> closing
- Structured AI pipelines with operator oversight
- Human + AI hybrid decision flow

### Tlaloc
Core intelligence layer for business understanding.

- Interprets operations and inefficiencies
- Drives structured, context-aware decisions
- Includes governance-oriented reasoning

### ERP / MES Systems
Manufacturing systems running in real production environments.

- Quotations, inventory, process flows, BOMs
- Multi-level operational workflows
- Ongoing migration to SaaS multi-tenant architecture
